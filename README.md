## About

Cybersecurity had me at "you can't protect what you don't understand." I like pulling systems apart to see how they tick — and how they break — because that's where the real answers live. Patterns, puzzles, the slow click of a problem finally making sense: that's the part I never get tired of.

What keeps me here is bigger than the code, though. I want people to feel safe in a world that's increasingly lived online, and I want my work to actually matter — not just technically sound, but genuinely useful to whoever it ends up protecting.

Hands-on is how I learn: auditing code, building SAST tooling, digging into cryptography, and grinding CTFs on TryHackMe, PortSwigger, CyLab and SEED Labs — clickjacking, broken auth, XSS, buffer overflows, all of it. I keep a pulse on the field through DarkReading and the Chaos Computer Club.

⚽ Off the keyboard, you'll find me cheering for Portugal.

🔎 Currently looking for a cybersecurity internship.
